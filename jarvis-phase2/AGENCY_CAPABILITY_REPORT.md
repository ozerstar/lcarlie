# L'CARLIE Creative & Growth Agency

Additive business capability built on the current Company Memory and
Specialist Pool contracts. It has no runtime trigger, provider implementation,
research call, external action, deployment, or production configuration.

Eight roles are metadata. Deterministic routing selects six for campaign work,
three for performance review, and three for social planning. Evidence is loaded
once into a shared snapshot and reused by every selected role.

Vireon copy stays inside a cosmetic care/support boundary. Medical treatment,
erection, delay, guaranteed performance, personal sexual-attribute targeting,
and unsupported outcome claims are prohibited. Commercially unconfirmed Meta
claims are explicitly excluded from sales truth.
